Konoba Empire

Build your Dalmatian estate.

Konoba Empire is a cozy, single-player idle tycoon. You inherit a crumbling stone estate on the Dalmatian coast and rebuild it into a thriving konoba (tavern), powered by your own vineyard and olive grove. The three streams feed each other — your tavern serves your own wine, and your olive oil enriches every dish.

What makes Konoba Empire different

★ Three interlocking streams. A vineyard (wine), a tavern (food), and an olive grove (oil) that cross-pollinate — grow one and the others get stronger.

★ A real collection to chase. Discover 252 recipes — 108 wines and 144 dishes. Basics reveal themselves as you grow; rare combinations you experiment toward. The recipe book is the heart of the game.

★ No timers, no energy, no subscriptions. A calm idle game for 5-minute dips and long offline gaps. Your estate keeps working while you're away.

★ Zero data collection. Your estate lives only on your device. No accounts, no sign-in.

★ One honest premium unlock. The optional Estate Charter doubles your earnings, removes ads, and keeps 24 hours of offline progress — once, no subscription.
