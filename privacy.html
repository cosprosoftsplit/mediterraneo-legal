Konoba Empire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-28

Konoba Empire ("the App") is a single-player iOS game developed by Cosmic Production / SoftSplit ("we", "us"). This page describes our privacy practices.

What we collect

Nothing. Konoba Empire does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not have user accounts. We do not require sign-in. We do not track you across other apps or websites for our own purposes.

Local data on your device

Your estate progress (coins, producers, recipes, prestige, settings) is stored only on your device using Apple's local data storage (SwiftData). This data is never uploaded to any server we operate. Deleting the app removes this data.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases (Estate Charter, Heirloom Pack, and coin bundles) are processed by Apple's StoreKit. We receive only a transaction receipt to validate your purchase. We never see your Apple ID, credit card, or billing information.

Advertising

If you have not purchased the Estate Charter, the App displays advertisements via Google AdMob. With your permission (App Tracking Transparency), AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads; if you decline, you will see non-personalized ads. AdMob's data practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can change your choice in your device Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Konoba Empire is rated 12+ for infrequent/mild references to alcohol (wine production is a theme). We do not knowingly collect any data from any user, including minors.

Changes to this policy

If we change our privacy practices, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the App Store update notes.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@cosmicproduction.hr.
