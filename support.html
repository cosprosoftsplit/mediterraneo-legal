Konoba Empire — Support

Got a question? Most answers are below. If you can't find what you need, email us at support@cosmicproduction.hr and we'll get back to you within 2 business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap to harvest your first vine, then buy more producers and hire managers to automate them. Your estate earns coins on its own — even while you're away. Spend coins to grow, discover recipes, and eventually "prestige" for a permanent speed boost.

What are the three streams?

Your estate has three interlocking income streams: a Vineyard (wine), a Tavern / konoba (food), and an Olive Grove (oil). They feed each other — a bigger vineyard makes your tavern more valuable, and your olive oil enriches every dish.

What is the Recipe Book?

It's the heart of the game: 252 recipes to discover — 108 wines and 144 dishes. Basic recipes reveal themselves as your production grows. Rarer combinations are unlocked by spending "Inspiration" at the craft bench. Discovering recipes permanently increases your earnings.

What does the Estate Charter include?

The Estate Charter (one-time purchase) doubles all your earnings, removes ads, raises your offline-earnings cap to 24 hours, and unlocks decorative estate crests. It is a one-time non-consumable purchase — no subscription.

Are coin bundles required to progress?

No. Coin bundles are optional accelerators. Nothing in the game is locked behind a purchase — you can discover all 252 recipes and reach every milestone for free.

I bought something but it's not showing.

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases to re-validate non-consumable purchases (Estate Charter, Heirloom Pack) with Apple. If that doesn't work, email support@cosmicproduction.hr with your transaction receipt.

Does Konoba Empire work offline?

Yes. The game is fully playable without internet, and your estate keeps earning while you're away (up to a cap). In-app purchases require connectivity only for the initial purchase, then restore from your local receipt.

Does the game collect my data?

No. We do not collect any personal data. All your progress is stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I sync my estate across devices?

iCloud sync is on our roadmap for a future update. For now, your estate is per-device.

Will there be an Android version, or other regions?

We're iOS-first. Other regional editions of the Mediterraneo series (Italian, Spanish, Greek, French) and an Android version are being considered for future releases.

How do I delete my game data?

Uninstalling Konoba Empire removes all local game data from your device. There is no server-side data to delete because we don't collect any.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email support@cosmicproduction.hr with as much detail as possible (device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue happened).
